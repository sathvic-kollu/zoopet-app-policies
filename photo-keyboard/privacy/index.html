Privacy Policy – Photo Keyboard: Sticker Maker

App: Photo Keyboard: Sticker Maker (package app.photokeyboard.stickermaker) · Publisher: Zoopet · Effective: 9 October 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Photo Keyboard: Sticker Maker ("the app") is published by Zoopet. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@zoopet.in.

2. What you type

Android keyboards can see the text you type. Photo Keyboard processes typing on your device to insert text, show suggestions and run the features you choose. We do not send what you type to us, to advertising or to analytics, and we do not keep a history of your conversations. In password fields and in private mode, suggestions, snippets and voice input are switched off. The apps you type into receive your text under their own policies.

3. Photos and themes

When you pick a photo for a keyboard theme, the app uses the Android photo picker and saves a private copy for editing; your original photo is never changed. Themes and settings stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

4. Clipboard and snippets

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap paste. There is no automatic clipboard history. Snippets are text you choose to save; unpinned snippets expire after one hour and pinned snippets stay until you delete them. Please do not save passwords or payment details as snippets.

5. Stickers and GIFs

Stickers and animated GIFs are created on your device from built-in characters, your text and emoji. They are not generated by a cloud service. When you share or insert a sticker, the receiving app gets a copy under its own policy. Temporary exports are kept for up to 24 hours.

6. Backups

You can export a backup file of your themes, photos, settings and pinned snippets to a location you choose. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them private. Purchases are not included in backups.

7. Microphone (optional voice typing)

Voice typing starts only when you tap the voice button and grant microphone permission. It uses your phone's on-device speech recognition (Android 12 or later). Audio is not recorded, stored or sent to us. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in Android settings.

8. Translation (optional)

The translator works on text you enter into it. Before first use of a language pair, the app asks you to confirm a one-time language-model download from Google (Wi-Fi only by default). Translation then runs on your device with Google ML Kit, and your text is not sent to us or to a cloud translation service. Google may process technical information needed to deliver the download and operate ML Kit under Google's terms. You can delete downloaded models in the app.

9. Advertising

The companion app (not the keyboard itself) shows ads: banner, interstitial, native and app-open ads. There are no ads inside the keyboard window. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use mediation partners Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Mintegral, Liftoff Monetize and InMobi. These providers may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and personalise ads and prevent fraud, under their own privacy policies. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads load, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.

10. Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play (Play version) or Samsung Galaxy Store (Galaxy version). We never receive your payment details. The app checks with the store on your device whether you own Remove Ads and keeps an encrypted note of the purchase on your device. No purchase receipt or token is sent to us or to any server we operate. Refunds and cancellations follow the store's own rules.

11. No analytics or crash reporting

The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting or push-notification services and does not upload usage logs. Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The Play version may read Google Play install-referrer information on the device; it is not uploaded by the app.

12. Data retention and deletion

Your themes, photos, snippets, stickers and settings are stored only on your device. Settings → Clear my data deletes this app data, and uninstalling the app removes it as well. Files you exported or shared elsewhere, and data held by advertising providers or app stores, are not removed by this and are subject to their own controls. There are no user accounts in the app.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, to object, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the app keeps your content on your device, most requests can be handled with the in-app controls above. For anything else, email contact@zoopet.in. Never send passwords or payment details by email.

14. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

15. Security

The app keeps data in private app storage, encrypts the purchase note with the Android Keystore and uses secure connections for network requests made by the Google and advertising SDKs. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

16. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date above.
