Privacy Policy – Bodyweight Home Workout

App: Bodyweight Home Workout (package app.bodyweight.homeworkout) · Publisher: Zoopet · Effective: 19 October 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Bodyweight Home Workout ("the app") is published by Zoopet. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@zoopet.in.

2. What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps your workout history (dates, which exercises you finished, workout length and level), your reminders, your settings and, if you use the BMI calculator, the weight and height you enter. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. We do not run servers for this app and nothing you enter is sent to us. There are no user accounts.

3. Health and fitness information

Workout history, weight and height are fitness information. They are used only on your device to show your progress, streaks and BMI. They are not shared with advertisers or anyone else, and they are not used for advertising.

4. Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your workout history and settings can be included in your Google account backup and restored to a new phone, under Google's terms. Advertising and consent choices are not included.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device using the times you choose. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications when you create a reminder. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings.

6. Voice coach

The voice coach uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine to read out exercise names and countdowns. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

7. Advertising

The app shows ads: banner, native, interstitial (only after you finish a workout) and app-open ads. No ads appear while a workout is running. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use the mediation partner Unity Ads. These providers may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, under their own privacy policies (Google, Unity). Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad loads, and you can change it later in Settings → Privacy and ad choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

8. Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play (Play version) or Samsung Galaxy Store (Galaxy version). We never receive your payment details. The app checks with the store on your device whether you own Remove Ads and remembers the result on your device. No purchase receipt or token is sent to us or to any server we operate. Refunds follow the store's own rules.

9. No analytics or crash reporting

The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting or push-notification services and does not upload usage logs.

10. Data retention and deletion

Settings → Reset progress deletes your workout history on this device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Data held by advertising providers or app stores is subject to their own controls.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, to object, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the app keeps your information on your device, most requests can be handled with the in-app controls above. For anything else, email contact@zoopet.in.

12. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. Health disclaimer

The app provides general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you have a health condition.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date above.
